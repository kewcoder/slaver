# SLAVER
### Don't Just Connect Software. Own It.

**[0:00–0:35 | THE PROBLEM]**

AI is changing the software industry.

Software development is becoming increasingly automated. This creates a serious challenge for developers: how do we continue creating value and earning from our skills in an AI-driven world?

At the same time, businesses face another problem.

Many business owners still don't know how to use AI or software to improve their operations.

Those who already use technology often depend on multiple SaaS subscriptions. They pay for software every month, but they don't truly own or control what they use.

We believe there is a better way.

**[0:35–1:00 | INTRODUCING SLAVER]**

Meet Slaver.

A marketplace connecting businesses with software creators, AI agents, and service providers.

Our vision is simple: help developers become independent software creators, while helping businesses own the technology they depend on.

**[1:00–1:30 | MARKETPLACE DEMO]**

Welcome to Slaver Marketplace.

Discover applications built by developers and AI agents.

Buy software, own your licensed copy, and customize it to fit your business.

Need something more specific?

Explore Services to find developers and agencies who can build new features, integrate systems, and maintain your applications.

**[1:30–2:15 | AI AGENTS DEMO]**

But Slaver goes beyond a traditional marketplace.

Your personal AI agent helps you understand your business needs and discover the right applications and services.

Once you own an application, Meta-powered AI agents can work together to understand its code, plan changes, build new features, and test them.

You can use your own AI agents, work with the original creator, or hire another developer.

You don't have to start from scratch, and you don't have to build an entire technical team.

**[2:15–2:40 | THE BIGGER VISION]**

And this is only the beginning.

Imagine a business connecting its software to AI cameras, smart sensors, POS terminals, and physical equipment through local service providers.

Slaver connects not only software and people, but eventually the entire business ecosystem.

**[2:40–3:00 | THE VISION]**

We believe AI should create more opportunities, not just automate existing jobs.

Developers become creators. Agencies reach more businesses. Business owners gain access to technology they can own and control.

Instead of simply renting more software, we can build a world where businesses own more of their technology, and creators earn from what they build.

**Slaver. Don't just connect software. Own it.**
